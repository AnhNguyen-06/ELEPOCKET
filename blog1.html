SO WHAT EVEN IS ELEPOCKET?

Welcome to the very first post on ELE-BLOG!

We - the ELEPOCKET team - are super excited to introduce to you who we are and what this project is all about. Let's get started!

The team

Founded by a group of Vietnamese students, ELEPOCKET is a website project with the mission of helping other students (including ourselves!) manage our personal finances. To make this happen, we have teamed up and contributed our best to the project, guided by our Board of Direction:

Nguyen Van Quang Anh

VNU-HCM University of Technology

Former student at VNU-HCM High School for the Gifted

Co-founder - Tech & Data

Tran Nguyen Bao Tran

VNU-HCM High School for the Gifted

Co-founder - PR & Product Research

Why ELEPOCKET?

It all started with a simple thought:

“We want to create something helpful not only for other students but also for ourselves!”

From that spark, we have been working hard to turn ELEPOCKET into a sustainable and practical tool - a true companion - for students to gain smarter budgeting and saving habits. Therefore, take our quick test right now to discover your type of spending and receive practical advice for your budgeting!

Stay tuned! You can never know our next moves :>
